# Keep learning

## Books and sites

- **The Little Book of Ruby** by Huw Collingbourne: a free PDF of short sample
  programs. Ten short chapters.
- **[rubycommunity.africa](https://rubycommunity.africa)**: Learning Materials,
  Events, Chapters and a "Built with Ruby" list.

## The African Ruby Community (ARC)

According to the community website, ARC started in 2010, has 5,000+ members
across East Africa, and holds weekly and monthly meetups. Chapters are listed
for Kenya (Nairuby), Rwanda, Tanzania and Uganda.

## Built with Ruby in Africa

Companies listed on the Built with Ruby page of rubycommunity.africa include
Zege Technologies, Senga Technologies, Quikk API, Uamuzi Foundation and
Branch International (Kenya), and Glucode and SimplePay (South Africa).
They work on things like freight and fleet software, payments and mobile money,
civic tech, and cloud payroll. Most of the listings link to a careers or
volunteer page, so real companies in Africa hire for this.

Check the page itself for the current list.
